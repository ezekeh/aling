The weekly rhythm

Seven days. One simple thing to do on each.

Growing on LinkedIn alone is hard, so we do it together. Tap a day to see what happens.

Today

  1. ALING Pledge

    Say it out loud, then do it.

    Members state their goals and commitments for the week. Once the circle has heard your plan, you have people to answer to.

  2. ALING Engagement Pond

    Your posts get real readers.

    A dedicated space to intentionally engage with and support one another's content.

  3. ALING Unleashed

    Play, compete, make friends.

    A day for friendly competitions and games. This is where a group of professionals becomes a circle of friends.

  4. ALING Skill Swap

    Trade what you know for what you need.

    ALING Giants exchange their skills for the ones they want in return.

  5. Engagement Pond 2

    A second push before the weekend.

    Another dedicated space to intentionally engage with and support one another's content.

  6. ALING Rising Giants of the Week

    Get celebrated in front of everyone.

    We celebrate and amplify 5 outstanding members. Next week, one of them can be you.

  7. ALING Lounge

    Rest. Giants recharge.

    ALING's day of rest. Breathe, reflect, and come back ready to pledge again on Monday.

Questions

Before you join.

Is it really free?

Yes. Joining ALING costs nothing. What we ask for is your activity: show up, take part and support other members.

How much is expected of me?

Each day has one focused activity, so you always know what to do. The ALING Call and the ALING Forum each happen once a month. We remain active, and we hold each other accountable.

I'm still new on LinkedIn. Can I join?

Yes. ALING exists for growing and promising LinkedIners. You don't need a big following; you need to be intentional about your growth.

What if it turns out not to be for me?

Leave the group with one tap. Nothing to cancel and nothing to pay.
